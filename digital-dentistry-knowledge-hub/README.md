# Digital Dentistry Knowledge Hub

A personal learning and research portfolio focused on digital dentistry, dental materials, and dental laboratory technology.

## About This Project

This repository documents my professional development as a dental technician. It combines literature reviews, technical documentation, and practical learning projects related to digital dentistry.

My goal is to build a structured knowledge base, improve my scientific reading and technical writing skills, and explore emerging technologies in dental laboratory workflows.

## Research Interests

- CAD/CAM dentistry
- Milled and 3D-printed denture materials
- Dental biomaterials and material properties
- Digital denture workflows
- Additive manufacturing and post-processing

## Current Projects

### 1. 3D-Printed vs. Milled Denture Base Materials

A literature review comparing the mechanical properties of additively manufactured and subtractively milled denture base materials.

**Topics of interest**
- Flexural strength
- Surface hardness
- Fracture toughness
- Impact strength
- Manufacturing and post-processing variables

**Status:** In progress

### 2. Dental Technology Glossary

An English-Korean glossary of technical terms used in dental laboratory workflows and digital dentistry.

**Status:** Planned

## Learning Approach

For each project, I aim to:

1. Identify reliable scientific and technical sources.
2. Extract and compare relevant findings.
3. Document limitations and unanswered questions.
4. Write clear technical summaries in English.
5. Record progress and reflect on what I have learned.

## Disclaimer

This repository is for educational and professional development purposes. Literature summaries are not original clinical research and should not be interpreted as clinical recommendations.

## Progress

This portfolio is a work in progress. Projects and documentation will be updated as I continue learning.
