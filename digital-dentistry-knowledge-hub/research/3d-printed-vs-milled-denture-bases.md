
# 3D-Printed vs. Milled Denture Base Materials

## 1. Research Question

How do 3D-printed and milled denture base materials compare in their mechanical properties?

## 2. Background

Digital dentistry uses both additive manufacturing (3D printing) and subtractive manufacturing (milling) to produce dental prostheses.

The manufacturing method may influence the mechanical properties and performance of denture base materials.

## 3. Key Properties to Investigate

- Flexural strength
- Surface hardness
- Fracture resistance
- Water sorption
